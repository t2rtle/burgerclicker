About Burger Clicker

An incremental game that is easy to start, but full of paths to grow your restaurant.

🎮 What is Burger Clicker?

Burger Clicker is an incremental game created by Crabscary. The idea is to produce burgers, improve your operation, and turn a small production into a true food empire.

📈 How does it work?

  • 🍔 Produce burgers by clicking and increasing your automatic production.
  • 🏪 Buy employees, equipment, food trucks, restaurants, and franchises.
  • ⬆️ Purchase upgrades to increase the efficiency of your operation.
  • ⭐ Perform Rebirths and use prestige progression to reach new levels.
  • 🐾 Find, equip, and evolve pets with their own rewards.
  • 🏆 Complete achievements and unlock rewards throughout your progression.

✨ Experience

The game is designed to run directly in the browser and also offers installable app (PWA) features in compatible browsers. Progress and several preferences are stored locally on the device.

🍔 Goal: produce more and more, grow your restaurant, discover new possibilities, and try to beat your own progress.
