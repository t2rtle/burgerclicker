Privacy Policy

Transparency about the data, storage, and technologies used by Burger Clicker.

💾 Game storage

Burger Clicker uses the browser's local storage, mainly localStorage, to keep the player's progress and preferences on this device.

These data are part of the game's operation and may be deleted when the site or browser data are removed.

📢 Advertising

Burger Clicker may display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, according to the applicable settings and policies.

Burger Clicker does not individually control the data processed by advertising providers. To understand how Google handles data related to advertising, please consult Google's own privacy and advertising policies.

🧑‍💻 Personal data

Burger Clicker does not directly request personal information to let you play. The game does not have a mandatory registration or player account system of its own.

External features, such as advertising services or platforms accessed by the player, may have their own data collection and processing practices.

🍪 Cookies and preferences

Cookies may be used by third-party services, including advertising. Cookie behavior depends on the service used, browser settings, and consent options available in your region.

🔄 Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when the game, its features, or the services it uses are modified. The version published on this page represents the policy currently presented by Burger Clicker.
